Accessibility statement

Friendly Poker is committed to accessibility and to the principles of Israeli Standard 5568, based on WCAG 2.0 AA.

The site provides keyboard navigation, screen-reader labels, scalable text, high contrast, grayscale, readable-font, link highlighting, and stop-animation options. We continue to improve accessibility across gameplay and account features.

For an accessibility request or difficulty, please contact the site administrator through the Friendly Poker Telegram group.
